## About Kling AI
Kling AI is a text-to-video generation model developed by Kuaishou Technology.
Released in 2024, Kling can generate videos up to 2 minutes long at 1080p resolution.
It uses a 3D spatiotemporal attention mechanism for coherent motion.

## Key Features
- Text-to-video generation
- Image-to-video generation
- Up to 120 seconds video length
- 1080p resolution output
- Realistic physics simulation

## Pricing
- Free tier: limited monthly generations
- Standard: paid plans with more generations and faster queue
